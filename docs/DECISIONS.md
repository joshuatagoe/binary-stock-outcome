# Decisions

Key decisions, why we made them, and what they cost. A new decision is added in the same commit as the work it affects.

Status is **Accepted** (agreed with the user), **Proposed** (awaiting the user), or **Superseded** (replaced; the entry stays and points to its replacement).

Product decisions (D-numbers) are added only after the user answers the question round that follows the stage 1 decision review. Process decisions about how the project is run use P-numbers, so the two series never collide.

The plan's own decisions keep their numbers (D1 to D30). An open question answered in the round becomes a new decision from D31 on. "From" names the plan item; finding IDs (F1.05 is finding 5 in group 1) point to `docs/research/reports/2026-10-09-full-G<n>.md`, and the judges' reports beside them give the options that were weighed.

## Assumptions

- **A1:** No protocol fees in version 1. Reason: the spec allows fees only in a separate account (F2.38), and comparable venues charge takers only, if at all (F2.32; F2.33, SECONDARY; F2.34); on devnet a fee adds rounding and P&L cases with no benefit. The design keeps a separate fee account possible, so fees can be added later without touching the vault. What it affects: D10's payoff line and every P&L figure. The review found it holds.
- **A4:** Money is whole numbers. USDC has 6 decimals ($1 = 1,000,000 units), read from the token's settings at start-up rather than assumed. Yes and No tokens have 0 decimals, so one token is one contract. Prices are whole cents from 1¢ to 99¢. A Pyth price is compared with a strike by scaling the strike up to Pyth's format ($680 becomes 68,000,000 at exponent −5), never by rounding Pyth's price; the exponent is read from every update. Reason: rounding $679.996 to $680.00 would turn a No into a Yes (F4.56), and the exponent's type differs between Pyth's services (F4.65, F4.66); devnet USDC's decimals weren't confirmed (F4.64, SECONDARY). What it affects: settle, every price on screen and every test. The review found it holds with these fixes.
- **A5:** The bot has its own operator key, stored in the config beside the admin key. It may create markets and settle normally; it may not pause or override. Reason: a leaked hot key on the server should not carry the admin's powers, and the bot already settles alone for the first 24 hours (D58). What it affects: D24's permission table and D34.
